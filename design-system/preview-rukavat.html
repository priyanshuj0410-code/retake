The worlds

Five printed worlds over one skeleton. Each brings its own paintings, objects and palette; the pick yellow and the focus ring are shared.

Lugdi लुगदी

Each day is the next issue of a 1970s Hindi pulp novel.

हर दिन सत्तर के दशक के एक हिंदी लुगदी उपन्यास का अगला अंक है।

Station स्टेशन

Each day is printed on Indian Railways enamel boards, tickets and charts.

हर दिन भारतीय रेल के इनेमल बोर्ड, टिकट और चार्ट पर छपा है।

Jadugar जादूगर

Dekhiye, dekhiye! Each day is one night of a travelling jadoo show in a small town.

देखिए, देखिए! हर दिन किसी छोटे शहर में आए जादू के शो की एक रात है।

Cricket क्रिकेट

Each day is a match at the old stadium under floodlights, called by the commentator.

हर दिन फ़्लडलाइट में पुराने स्टेडियम का एक मैच है, कमेंटेटर की ज़ुबानी।

Rukavat रुकावट

Each day is an evening in front of the 1980s family TV, until the announcer says "Rukavat ke liye khed hai".

हर दिन अस्सी के दशक के घर के टीवी के सामने एक शाम है, जब तक उद्घोषक न कहे, "रुकावट के लिए खेद है"।

The report over time

The report never says which way a player leans. Early numbers are pulled towards zero, so day one is never harsh. A change counts only when the lean stays below the first two weeks by more than its normal wobble, three Sundays running. The exact line comes from the research (RSE-164), not from design. It is a change in the game's picks, never a claim about the person.

Every screen size

One page, built once, that rearranges itself by the space it gets, and the painting fills the screen at every size. Phones and upright tablets use the portrait painting. Anything wider than tall uses a wide painting composed with its subject on the left, and the game sits in a column over the open right side, on a soft dark fade, never a blur. The cards turn on their side when there is room, and the tabs become a rail.

Hindi, large text and small phones

Your choices

Each open question drawn both ways. The default stands until you choose. The first two would change a rule in the rulebook.

Where the day plate sits (a rule change)

The rulebook says the top bar holds only back and "?". Putting the plate in the bar keeps every step the same height and carries the world into the first view.

Small phones (a rule change)

Four people with their lines cannot fit 360 x 640 without scrolling. The rule asks for no scroll.

Who says the loaded lines

Each set gets one named local voice with a portrait. You can rename them.

The twin wording

The equal pair differs only by one loaded word. A later day swaps the words between them.

Text over the paintings

Text needs a plate or a tint to read over a painting.
