Equal Lens Daily design system

One game, drawn by one hand, in two printed worlds that switch every week. Every part below has one meaning and is drawn in every state, in both worlds. Nothing in the app may use a part that is not on this page. Tap the cards and buttons in the first two phones: they work.

One skeleton under both worlds

  1. 1
    Top barBack at the left, help at the right. On the cover: streak count, language chip, help. Nothing else.
  2. 2
    The day's objectLugdi: the painting fills the phone behind everything. Station: the board or chart heads the screen.
  3. 3
    The questionOne line of at most 10 words, always in the same place. It is the screen's heading.
  4. 4
    3 to 5 peopleIdentical before the choice, in a shuffled order. A twin pair hides among them: here Kamla and Raju have the same record in different words.
  5. 5
    One main buttonPinned to the bottom, the same size and place every day. It waits with what is missing, then turns yellow and names the consequence.

Your choices

Where a rule and an approved sample disagree, both are drawn here. The default applies until you choose.

OQ-SB: Station name board colour

Yellow now means only your pick. Real station boards are yellow.

OQ-3: Button lettering in Lugdi

Hindi has no capitals, and the display face is hard to read small.

OQ-4: End of the day in Lugdi

You once called a result "very discouraging and terminal".

OQ-5: Cover roundel

On a free game a price can read as a charge.

OQ-6: Third script on Station boards

You chose to show only the chosen language.

OQ-12/13: Naming the day's trick or a transfer day

The brief says test days are never announced.

Rules the rulebook took strictly, for you to confirm

  • Play, Continue and Share are not yellow, because they confirm no pick.
  • The Sunday look-again pair never marks which person you picked, because that would show your direction for that pair.
  • Stat labels show once for the set, not on every card, to stay inside the word limit.
  • No display face on puzzle steps; the object carries the day.
  • Under reduced motion, changes are instant cuts, not fades.

Signature moves

Lugdi: the result reprints the cover, red ink, then black, then paper white. Station: figures arrive on split-flap tiles. Under reduced motion both are instant.
