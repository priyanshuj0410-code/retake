Retake · stages 2 and 3 · updated 22 Oct 2026

Features and screen map

A daily puzzle that shows you your two ways: open a link, play a short puzzle at your level on a printed object, see your score and the size of your lean, share a card that is safe in a family group, and on Sunday look again at where you judged the same thing two ways. Or start with the First take, an optional 20-minute starter whose private results show how your reading was made, and take it again each month as the Next take.

What changed since the last version of this page

  • The game is called Retake (रीटेक). The 20-minute starter is the First take, and each monthly redo the Next take.
  • The First take is in the first version: five chapters of about 4 minutes, one per world, with private results that show which way the choices leaned.
  • Three dailies a day, Easy, Medium and Hard, each level with its own Today, This week and All time boards.
  • Five worlds take a week each: Lugdi, Station, Jadugar, Cricket and Rukavat. Three new kinds of decision: Believe, the batting order and Split.
  • Sign out for a saved player; signing in on a new phone signs the old one out. "Wrong date?" once on the wait screen.
  • The metal look with fewer, finer borders, and each world telling its own story, are design decisions: they live in the design document, not here.

How to read the feature tables
  • A row is one thing a player gets or can do, or one thing the game deliberately never does. It names where it lives, who needs it (the personas P1 to P6 in personas.md) and when it ships.
  • First version? V1 is needed for the first release the owner puts in front of players. Later is designed after V1. Never is a deliberate non-goal, listed again with its reason under "Never".
  • Basis points to journey stages S1 to S15 and moments M1 to M5 in journeys.md, rule ids (R-...) in the consolidated rules, the owner's decisions O1 to O13 (O11 Qn is the owner's answer to question n of 21 Oct 2026; O12 is the starter and difficulty decision of the same day; O13 is the game's name, Retake, with the starter's screen names; D1 to D9 are the defaults set with the O11 answers and D10 to D18 those set with O12, all listed under "Owner answers"), sections of DESIGN.md and PRODUCT.md (PROD), digest ids (DG, UA, HIG, UXW, PH), and the journeys' old question numbers (OQ-n). The critique is critique-stage1.md.
  • Status. Decided means settled by an owner decision, a binding rule or DESIGN.md, or by a default this file sets for an old question not worth the owner's time now (Basis names the default). Decided (default, owner to confirm) means the row rests in part on one of the defaults D1 to D18 set with the owner's answers and decisions of 21 Oct 2026; it stands unless the owner says otherwise. No row waits on a question. From the research line in Basis marks a part the research line still owes (listed under "Owner answers"); the row stands and only that part waits. Open means the owner has left it open and it is not asked now; the tag shown is where it would sit if it ever comes.
  • Conflicts. The rules file's order holds: the owner, then the rules, then the digests. Where the fortnight content plan or the drawn library breaks a rule, the rule wins and Basis says so.
  • Not asked again: the look-and-feel choices already open in DESIGN.md "Open owner choices". Their defaults are used here.

Every screen

On a phone the game has three tabs, Today, Board and Week, in a fixed bar at the bottom (R-TAB-1); on anything wider than tall a 96 px rail on the left holds the same three in the same order, and the game sits in a column on the right over the full painting (DESIGN Larger screens). A phone's very first visit opens on one birth-date screen (SC-15), the only screen ever placed before the cover (O11 Q6, which overrides R-COVER-1 on a first visit only); someone under 18 stays on a screen that says in how many days they can play (SC-16), and everyone else goes on to the cover. Today opens on the cover, the home and the first screen of every later visit that starts from a link (R-COVER-1, F-01). Play turns the day into a full-screen sequence with no tabs: one puzzle step after another, back at the top left and "?" at the top right (R-TOP-1), until the last commit lands on the result, where the tabs come back. Under Play, the first-visit cover offers the starter as a quieter second action (SC-90, D10): five chapters of about 4 minutes, one in each world, each a full-screen sequence like a day that opens on its title card (SC-91) and ends on a between-chapters screen (SC-92) where the player can stop, and after the fifth a short sequence of cards with the player's private reading (SC-93); after the first visit the starter lives on the Week tab, and later the monthly Next take (SC-94). From the first done cover, a level switch on the cover (SC-95) picks Easy, Medium or Hard, three dailies with their own Play, Continue and done, and the Board has the same switch on all three periods, because each level keeps its own Today, This week and All time (SC-96, D13). The first result offers to save the player's days with an email and a 6-digit code (SC-17); the Board and a new phone need it, and play never does (D3). Once today's puzzle is done, and on Sundays, the cover also opens Past puzzles (SC-27), the earlier days this player has not played (D6). Sunday has no puzzle of its own, so the cover's button opens the week. The Board ranks Today, This week and All time, among friends who have added each other back or among everyone who has joined. The "?" sheet is the one settings place (R-SHEET-4): it opens on how to play today's puzzle, and one level below it holds Settings, Road name, Privacy, Your numbers and About. From any screen with a top bar but the birth-date screen, "?" is one tap; from any tab screen, the other tabs are one tap; back is one tap on every full screen and sheet level. The language chip is one tap too, but only from the cover and the birth-date screen (R-LANG-1; FLAG under Navigation rules).
