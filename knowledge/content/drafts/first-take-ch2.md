In Lugdi 2 and 6 nothing happens beyond a car following a truck at night: no threat is spelled out, and the cover is the empty road with headlights far behind one truck, never a figure. Only Vikram seth doubts a report; every report on the cards is said plainly, and no card says it was nothing.
