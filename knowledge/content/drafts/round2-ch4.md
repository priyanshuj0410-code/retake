**The hard topic, with care.** The captain's stage of each batting order carries a police complaint of violence at home: against Chandan in Cricket 2, against Leena in Cricket 6, told in other words of the same weight. Both are new, play only in this chapter and carry no community mark, so none of the eight who come back in every world carries it. Zarina and Uday, the other two new faces, never carry it. Who made the complaint is never said; no one at home is named or drawn; nothing of what happened is told. The art is the captain's armband on the bench, never a home or the police. Meena says only the fact, with no view on it, so there is nothing slanted for another card to answer. The woman's half is played exactly as straight as the man's, with no joke and no "henpecked" tone. Suggested, as in the cast plan: result cards 2 and 3 never draw on this twin.

**Whether the two halves are the same fact.** Many Indian players may read a complaint of violence at home against a woman as a counter-case in a family quarrel with the in-laws, not as the same fact as the one against a man. Keep this twin only if the five Hindi-first players read both halves as the same fact. The research line records the difference either way.
