# Ship-readiness UX audit, 19 October 2026

Audited build: `main` at 924237e, demo mode. Design system: [../design.md](../design.md).
Method copied from Nivel (`parity-tracker/knowledge/reviews/ux-audit-2026-10-02.md` and
`design-audit-2026-10.md`). **Status:** every blocker, major and minor is fixed except those
that change a registered design item, which are listed as [owner decisions](#owner-decisions).

## Verdict

| Release | Ready? | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| **Guided demo** (MIT Solve walkthroughs) | **Yes** | Nothing blocking after the fixes. |
| **Pilot with real players** | **No** | Owner decisions O1 to O3, the placeholder statistics and the identity pools' pre-test (both already marked in the content), and the manual screen reader pass below. |
| **Accessible launch** (WCAG 2.2 AA) | **Automated: yes** | Every automated count is zero on 141 screen visits at six viewports. A VoiceOver and TalkBack pass is still owed. |

Before the fixes the build was sound on the things that are hardest to retrofit: no sideways
scroll at 320 px, every target at least 44 px on phones, every control named, no text under
12 px, dark mode right, live regions on the ranking and allocation cards. What held it back
was screen structure (no heading on 29 screen types, one title for every page, focus dropped
after each card was confirmed), keyboard focus lost at the ends of the ranking list and on a
spent allocation, and five content and logic defects found by the owner's reviewer, two of
which touch research integrity (a scoring hint before release, and a withdrawal close date two
weeks early).

## Findings

### Blockers

| ID | Finding | Status | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | The season result said "Your people decisions shaped the story; the season was decided by the budget, vendor and schedule calls", telling every player before release that people decisions are not what the game scores (reviewer's b). | Fixed | The sentence is gone from `content/screens.json` and the result screen; the band line stays. |
| B2 | Withdrawal dates disagreed: the code screen said "until 15 March 2027", the closed page "closed on 2027-03-01", which is the release date, two weeks early, and was the first deployment's date whatever the code (reviewer's e). The explanation's not-yet notice also showed a raw ISO date. | Fixed | `releaseOf` and `formatDate` in `src/engine/config.ts` are the one source for the code screen, the explanation and withdrawal pages and the demo store. The closed page uses the code's own deployment: "Withdrawal closed on 15 March 2027". `pnpm check` (X-7) fails if anything else reads the dates. |

### Majors

| ID | Finding | Status | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Focus fell to the page after Confirm on every ranking and allocation card (20 screen visits), after the recall item, and after a withdrawal, because the continuation reused the screen's key (WCAG 2.4.3). | Fixed | A continuation is a new `stepKey`, so focus moves to its heading. |
| M2 | Keyboard: at the end of the ranking list and when the allocation pool was spent, the button became `disabled` and focus dropped to the page (5 of 8 runs). | Fixed | The move buttons and steppers are `aria-disabled` and do nothing at their limit; focus stays. |
| M3 | 29 screen types had no `h1`: every screening, survey, recall, probe, prior, Off the Record and Inbox item, the firm's language choice and the refused-link page. | Fixed | `Choice heading` puts the question in an `h1` inside its legend, wording unchanged; the refused-link page has a title. |
| M4 | Every page was titled "Team season" (WCAG 2.4.2). | Fixed | The document title is "`h1` · Team season". |
| M5 | Pronoun slips in conduct cards: "they was slow", and sentences restarting in lower case ("...laughed. she asked her once to stop") (reviewer's a). | Fixed | In the generator (`fill` in `src/engine/text.ts`): a gendered word takes the verb after it ("they were") and a capital at a sentence start. `content-check` fills all 56 gendered texts three ways and fails on any slip. |
| M6 | Game 2's explanation said which note a player got depended on screening answers, but game 2 has no screening block (reviewer's d). | Fixed | Game 2's paragraph now says the note and the extra statements were each drawn at random and did not depend on any answer. Game 1's is unchanged. |

## Deliberately not copied

- **A skip link.** Nothing focusable comes before `<main>`, and focus lands on the heading
  on every screen. `pnpm ux` fails if that ever changes.
- **Nivel's announce-the-title live region.** Moving focus to the `h1` already announces each
  screen; a second announcement would double it.
- **Raising the inactive glyphs to 4.5:1.** That would make them look pressable.
- From the benchmark: preset amounts, sliders, scroll-and-checkbox consent gates, in-card
  reorder animations and scores beside ranked rows (see above).

## How it was tested

- **Viewports:** 412 × 915 (Pixel 9), 320 × 800 (the WCAG reflow width) and 1280 × 900, light
  and dark, with reduced motion so axe never reads mid-fade (Nivel's lesson). One viewport at a
  time on one page: the script resizes and re-checks each screen in turn, never in parallel.
- **Screens:** 141 visits covering every screen type. Home; panel game 1 played until every
  branch was met (low scorers' survey, note, form B and recall; high scorers' Off the Record
  with M1-H's statement route; R1's partial arm); game 2 in Hinglish and in English (format
  practice, crosswise items, the Committee Inbox and its content note, ending in a withdrawal at
  session end); firm enrolment and session (disclosure, consent, language, ranking, allocation,
  conduct and rematch cards, direct block); every wrap screen (probe, result, prior, debrief,
  withdrawal code, demographics, submit); the explanation page (form, before release, open);
  the withdrawal page (form, done, closed) and a refused link.
- **Automated checks** (`pnpm ux`, in `pnpm check`): `scripts/ux-audit.ts` drives the demo
  build with Playwright and `scripts/lib/ux-checks.js`, Nivel's checker adapted to run on the
  current screen (the evaluator's screens are steps of one session, not routes). It measures
  computed text contrast against the real background, targets (44 px phones, 24 px desktop,
  a radio's whole label row counted as its target), accessible names, heading order and one
  `h1`, sideways overflow, the document title, a skip link where needed, live regions on the
  ranking and allocation cards, text under 12 px, axe-core 4.10.3 (`wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`,
  `wcag21a`, `wcag21aa`, `wcag22aa`, `best-practice`), focus on the new screen's heading, and
  keyboard runs of each ranking and allocation card (Tab to a control, Enter or Space to the
  end, focus kept). `content-check` fills every gendered text three ways; `release-check`
  holds the dates to one source. To rerun: `pnpm ux before|after` writes to `assets/`; plain
  `pnpm ux` writes `out/ux-audit.json`.
- **Task walkthroughs** at 412 px, recorded in the after file under `walkthroughs`:
  1. game 1 four times, to every branch, each submitted;
  2. game 2 in Hinglish, submitted, and in English, withdrawn at session end;
  3. firm enrolment, then the session in its window, submitted;
  4. the explanation with a game 2 code before release ("This page opens on 1 March 2027")
     and after (the corrected paragraph);
  5. withdrawal with a code (records deleted), and after the window with a game 1 code
     ("Withdrawal closed on 15 March 2027", matching the code screen's "until 15 March
     2027").
- **Content defects (a) to (f)** came from the owner's reviewer; each was confirmed in the
  source before fixing.

## What still needs a manual pass

Tools cannot judge these; they need a person with VoiceOver (iOS Safari) and TalkBack (Android
Chrome, ideally an older phone):

- that the `h1` inside a legend reads once, not twice, with the radio group;
- the ranking card: the drag by touch with TalkBack on, and that "Dot, Position 2 of 4" is
  heard after each move;
- the allocation card: that the remaining count is heard after each step, and how an
  `aria-disabled` plus is announced;
- reading order on the Inbox screens (incident, two questions, the Internal Committee route);
- Hinglish read by an English voice: whether `lang="hi-Latn"` helps or hurts pronunciation.
