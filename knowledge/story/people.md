# People and personas

Ages and dates come from [timeline.md](timeline.md). How family members may be options is in [family-as-options.md](family-as-options.md). No surnames anywhere: a surname marks caste or community. Names are first names, or what the family calls someone.

## The family

The family are the story. They are introduced in cutscenes before they take any part. Never on a hard decision; never in a matched pair.

### Asha, "Dadi" (आशा, दादी)

- **Born** 1948 in Manmad. Railway clerk from 1970; retired in 2008 as an office superintendent.
- **Persona:** sharp, dry, warm underneath. Remembers everything and forgives slowly. Has kept a diary since she was 18, the source of the finale. She was often the only woman in the room, and she noticed everything said there.
- **Speech:** short sentences, a dry joke at the end. Never preaches. Calls the player "beta".
- **Look:**
  - 1971: a thin plait, a cotton sari with a railway badge.
  - 1999: spectacles on a chain, her hair going grey.
  - 2026: white hair in a bun, a cardigan over a cotton sari, a steel watch she never takes off. The watch is her signature in every era.
- **Role:** narrator of the First take, and of every chapter she was present for. **Never a card, never a voice in a decision.** She tells; others ask.

### Vikram (विक्रम)

- **Born** 1978; Asha's son. An engineer in Nashik in 2026.
- **Persona:** keeps the peace, mostly. Softer than his mother and teases her gently.
  - At 14 (1992): the club's serious scorer, who repeats everything the coach says.
- **Look:** 1992, a too-big school sweater and a pencil behind his ear. 2026, a kurta over jeans; he looks younger than 48, with thick black hair greying only at the temples and a short beard (owner, 23 Oct 2026: "Vikram looks too old").
- **Role:**
  - 1992: the gossip voice ("Coach told me...").
  - 1999: **the family option** in the Split, outside the pair (see [family-as-options.md](family-as-options.md)).
  - 2026: a frame voice.

### Meera (मीरा)

- **Born** 1980 in Pune; Vikram's wife since 2002. Runs a tailoring business from home.
- **Persona:** hears everything first. Fond of Dadi and not afraid of her.
- **Role:** a voice in the 2026 frame only. She joins the family in 2002, so she is in no memory.

### Tara (तारा)

- **Born** 2004; Vikram and Meera's daughter, a final-year student.
- **Persona:** asks what nobody asks, and is impatient with "that's how it was".
- **Role:** the one who asks in the frame, and she inherits the trunk with the player. She is never the player: the player stays an unnamed decider.

### Mohan (मोहन)

- **Born** 1950; Asha's younger brother.
- **Life:** a steam fireman at Manmad from 1969. He feared the new engines would end his trade. He missed the new-engine course and left the railways in 1975 to drive for Bauji. In 2026 he still lives in Manmad.
- **Persona:** funny, proud, quick to take offence. Sings in the engine cab.
- **Look:** 1971, a fireman's vest and soot on his forearms. 1977, a driver's cap pushed back.
- **Role:**
  - 1971: **the family option** in the new-engine course ranking, outside the pair.
  - 1977: the gossip voice at the depot ("Mohan says...").

### Ramesh (रमेश)

- **Born** 1944; Bauji's son, Asha's husband from 1973. A truck driver who ran the family trucks from 1989. He died in 2015.
- **Persona:** quiet, steady, a man who hums old film songs on night runs.
- **Look:** a moustache and a checked shirt, sleeves rolled.
- **Role:**
  - 1977: **the family option** in the night convoy picks, outside the pair.
  - 1984 and 1992: present in cutscenes.
  - 2026: a photo on the wall. His death is never a plot device.

### Bauji (बाऊजी)

- **Born** 1920; Ramesh's father, owner of four trucks on the Kasara ghat. He died in 1989.
- **Persona:** gruff, generous, old-fashioned. He speaks in pronouncements: "A truck is a family's honour." He says the loaded things at the depot, as an old man of his time would, and is not a villain.
- **Role:** 1977, **the voice asking the questions**.

### Badi Amma (बड़ी अम्मा)

- **Born** 1924; Bauji's wife, Asha's mother-in-law. She died in 2003.
- **Persona:** runs the house from her chair. Shrewd, funny, set in her ways, and loves the TV serials.
- **Role:** 1999, **the voice asking the questions** at home, aged 75.

### Pallavi (पल्लवी)

- **Born** 1962; Asha's cousin.
- **Life:** in 1984 she ran off to be the Chandni Mandal's assistant, to her family's horror. She left the show in 1986 and became a teacher in Pune.
- **Persona:** bold and theatrical, a good storyteller.
- **Role:** 1984, **the gossip voice** in the troupe ("Pallavi heard...").
  - She is never an option. Every Jadugar decision is a single pick, and a family option there would cost the reading (see [family-as-options.md](family-as-options.md)).

### Rani (रानी)

- **Born** 1974; Asha's daughter.
- **Life:**
  - 1992: she plays for the club, the only year she does.
  - 1999: her wedding is fixed.
  - Married that December and moved to Pune.
- **Persona:** competitive and loyal, and she rolls her eyes at her mother's rules.
- **Role:**
  - 1992: **the family option** in the team picks for the final, outside the pair. She is 18 that year, so an adult.
  - 1999: the gossip voice of the wedding week.

**About Gurpreet.** A woman station master in 1971 was rare. She is written as the first woman to hold the post on the section. She is proud, strict, and harder on the women than on the men, which makes her remarks believable without making her a villain. *(fact-check: whether women held such posts then; if it reads as impossible, she becomes the assistant station master.)*
