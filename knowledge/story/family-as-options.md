# Family members as options

Owner, 23 Oct 2026: "put special emphasis on how we assign family members as options so they don't dilute the test". The family members are in [people.md](people.md).

## The problem

Every decision hides a matched pair: one woman and one man with exactly the same numbers. The reading comes from which of the two the player favours, across the two halves of a twin. A family member is a face the player knows and cares about. If they sit on a card, two things can go wrong:

1. **They steal the pick.** In a "pick one", the player picks the brother because he is the brother. Neither of the pair is picked, so that decision gives no reading. Done often, a chapter gives none at all.
2. **They tilt the pair.** If the family member were one of the pair, liking or disliking them would read as a lean. That is the leak the twin design exists to stop.

## The rules

1. **Never in the matched pair.** A family member is never one of the two with matching numbers.
2. **Only in kinds that compare the pair with each other.** The reading must survive whatever the player does with the family member.

   | Kind | How the pair is read | Family member allowed? |
   |---|---|---|
   | Rank | whoever of the two ranks higher | Yes: the pair still has an order |
   | Batting order | whoever of the two bats higher | Yes |
   | Pick a team | whether exactly one of the two is in | Yes: they take one seat, and the others still decide the pair |
   | Split | chores to her minus chores to him | Yes: they take chores, and the pair still differ |
   | Pick one, Believe | which of the two is picked | **No**: picking them leaves no reading |
   | Spend checks | whose report is taken up | **No**: reports are like single picks |

3. **In both halves of one twin.** The two halves of a twin must be built alike. The same family member sits in both halves, in the same position: same seats, same numbers band. Both halves are then tilted the same way, and the tilt cancels.
   - This is the one exception to "the two halves share no person". That rule protects the pair, and the family member is never in it.
4. **Numbers second best.** The family member's numbers sit between the strongest card's and the pair's.
   - Not the best, so choosing them is a real judgement and not a free pick.
   - Above the pair, so a player who follows the numbers takes the strongest card and the family member, and the last seat is a choice between the two of the pair. That keeps the reading, instead of letting both of the pair in or both out.
   - In a Split, they sit between the pair and the weakest person, so they take a middling share.
5. **Never in a hard twin or a "matters" twin.** Hard twins carry the most sensitive readings, and "matters" twins are where following the detail is the sound rule. A known face must not muddy either.
6. **At most one family member, in one twin, per chapter.** This keeps the instrument's other three twins untouched.
7. **Their note is neutral.** A family member's card note is plain ("Asha's brother. Fires the old engines."). It is never a loaded word, positive or negative.
8. **Gossip spreads.** When a family member is the gossip voice, the gossip touches at least two other cards besides the pair. A remark must never mark the pair as the only people being talked about (change 6, decoys).
9. **The speaker is never a card in their own decision.** The family member who asks the question (Bauji, Badi Amma) is never on its cards.

Four of five chapters. Every one is in a comparing kind, in both halves of a plain twin, never hard and never "matters".

## What it costs, and what to measure

- **Cost:** in a team pick, a player who always takes the family member has one seat fewer. The pair can then end up both in or both out more often, which gives no reading. Rank, the batting order and Split lose nothing.
- **What the research check looks at** (after the usage reset, with the decoy notes): whether the twins with a family option give readings as often as twins without one. If not, the family option moves out of team picks into rank, order and Split only.

## The check, for every decision

- [ ] Is a family member on the cards? If so, the kind is rank, batting order, team or Split.
- [ ] They are not in the matched pair.
- [ ] The twin is plain, not hard or "matters".
- [ ] The same family member is in the other half, in the same position.
- [ ] Their numbers sit between the strongest card's and the pair's (in a Split, between the pair and the weakest).
- [ ] Their note is neutral.
- [ ] They were introduced in a cutscene earlier in the take.
- [ ] They are 18 or over that year ([timeline.md](timeline.md)).
- [ ] No other twin in the chapter has a family member.
