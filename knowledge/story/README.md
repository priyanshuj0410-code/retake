# Retake's story bible

Owner, 23 Oct 2026: "Let's build the whole narrative of the cutscenes, questions, options, illustrations etc for the first take... Build knowledge base files for situations, timelines, people and their personas so that the story and the cast are consistent and we don't introduce plotholes."

## The files

| File | What it holds | Check it when |
|---|---|---|
| [timeline.md](timeline.md) | Every dated event, family and town; everyone's age in every year a story touches | You set a story in a year, or mention anything that happened before it |
| [people.md](people.md) | The family, with their personas, speech and roles; the town's people of each era; who may be a card, a voice or neither | You give anyone a line, a card or a portrait |

The story's premise and its scaling plan are in [../content/narrative-draft-2026-10-23.md](../content/narrative-draft-2026-10-23.md). This folder supersedes the earlier [../content/story.md](../content/story.md), the "one monsoon night", for everything except its research guardrails.

## The rules that keep it consistent

1. **One source.** A fact about a person, place or year lives in exactly one of these files. Anything that uses it points here; nothing restates it differently.
2. **Ages follow the timeline.** Before using anyone in a year, read their age in [timeline.md](timeline.md). Adults only on any card: 18 or over that year.
3. **Introduced before used.** A family member appears in a cutscene, with face, name and one line, before they speak in a decision or sit on a card.
4. **One role per person per era.** A person keeps one job and one place in an era, and changes only through events written in the timeline. No one is a truck driver in one chapter and a magician in the next.
5. **The family never decides the reading.** Family members follow [family-as-options.md](family-as-options.md): never in a matched pair, never in a hard or "matters" decision, and only in kinds that compare the pair with each other.
6. **No outcome depends on a pick.** Chapter closings are about the event. What really happened back then arrives only in the diary, after the last decision.
7. **History is checked.** Every real-world fact in a script (dates, places, how a railway or a tournament worked) is marked "fact-check" until checked against a source. Nothing political: no parties, elections, strikes or the Emergency. No caste, religion or community markers. No surnames.
8. **Style is the era.** A scene is painted in the art of its year ([situations.md](situations.md)). The present day has its own style.
9. **New story, new entries first.** A new chapter, daily or event chapter adds its people, dates and places to these files before its script is written.
