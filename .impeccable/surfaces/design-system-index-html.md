# Surface brief: Equal Lens Daily design system and component library

Scope: a review page that shows the whole design system: tokens, type, every component in every state, the day objects of both worlds, and sample screens composed only from those parts. Mode: Read for the page chrome (the owner studies it), Experience inside every phone frame (the world leads).
Audience and job: the owner reviews and approves the system before any app code; later builders build only from it.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md; knowledge/design-rules-consolidated.md; owner decisions of 21 Oct 2026 (yellow means only the pick and its confirm, 3 to 5 people per choice, pick then confirm, lean as a number every day with no direction). Open look questions are shown as side-by-side A/B pictures for the owner to choose.

## Direction contract

THESIS: one game drawn by one hand in two printed worlds. Each day is a real object (a pulp cover, a reservation chart, a ticket, a departure board) that the puzzle happens on, never a form of stacked boxes on a dark ground with art as a strip.
OWN-WORLD: Lugdi: night violet ground, a full-bleed gouache cover painting, misregistered two-ink title lettering (red and black under paper white) in Sarpanch, newsprint paper plates. Station: platform black, blue and white enamel boards with chipped edges, Edmondson ticket card, amber-free split-flap rows. Shared: Hind for all reading, one pick yellow, ink and paper text only.
STORY: the owner sees that every part has one meaning, every state is drawn, both worlds share one skeleton, and the day objects keep the samples' vibe at phone size with legible type.
FIRST VIEWPORT: the page opens on the two worlds side by side as full phone frames of the same day, the cover and the station board each carrying a live puzzle step, with the shared skeleton annotated beside them.
FORM: pinned by the owner (Lugdi and Station alternating weekly), list position 1; seed: none (roll skipped: the owner pinned the form in their own words on 20 Oct 2026, "lugdi, station and hind", then "change every week... go ahead"; a user-pinned direction beats the roll, per new-work.md).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
